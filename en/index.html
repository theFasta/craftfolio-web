The notebook of your creative workshop

Blade and pressure for every material, heat press temperatures and times, the steps of every project: everything you used to write on paper, organized and always with you.

Coming soon, for Android. In English and Italian.

What it does

Three sections

Equipment, Materials and Projects: your cutting machine and heat press, vinyl and cardstock, what you make step by step.

Linked cards

Link a project to the materials and tools it uses. Open a material and see every project that uses it.

Photos and links

Photos of your work, from the camera or the gallery, and links to tutorials, videos and suppliers.

Search everywhere

Find any card in a second, across all sections at once.

Automatic backup

Your cards and photos are saved to your own Google Drive, in a hidden folder only Craftfolio can see.

Private

No account, no ads, no tracking. Works offline.
