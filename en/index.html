The notebook of your creative workshop

Blade and force for every material, heat press temperatures and times, the steps of every project: everything you used to write on paper, organized and always with you.

For Android, in English and Italian.

What it does

Four sections

Equipment, Materials, Projects and Ideas: your cutting machine and heat press, vinyl and cardstock, what you make step by step, what inspires you.

Linked cards

Link a project to the materials and tools it uses. Open a material and see every project that uses it.

Photos and links

Photos of your work, from the camera or the gallery, and links to tutorials, videos and suppliers.

To do

Jobs and orders with quantity, due date and customer, ticked as done and delivered.

Your order

Sort each section by date, from A to Z or by hand: hold a card and drag it.

Search everywhere

Find any card in a second, across all sections at once.

Backup and sync

Cards and photos are saved to your own Google Drive. Phone and tablet with the same account stay in step.

Free to start

Unlimited equipment and materials, 3 projects, 3 ideas and 3 open to-dos. The full version removes every limit.

Private

No account, no ads, no tracking. Works offline.
